## university

Create one finished portrait 3:4 editorial paper collage artwork for the EDUCATION chapter of a personal portfolio website, adapting the Gathered Scenes Zine style. The first reference is the actual campus photograph; the second is a style reference only, do not copy its cathedral. Flat scanned warm ivory cotton paper, approximately 35% natural photographic fragments and 65% spacious cobalt-blue risograph/woodcut interpretation. Compose a strong tower in the right middle, quiet pale sky in the upper third, lake and architectural reflections below. Art only: ALL website text will be rendered as live HTML, so NO text, numbers, logos, titles, UI, captions, microtext or border anywhere.

Preserve the photographed Xiamen University of Technology campus identity: the tall narrow light-colored tower with its sloping top on the right, broad stepped building on the left, mountain ridge behind, and lake in front. Keep the tower facade photographic and naturally lit. Simplify the mountain and other buildings into a few calm printed masses. Merge dense foliage into two shapes; no individual leaves. Let the exact architectural verticals, roof slopes and lake horizon organize the piece.

A cobalt-blue ink field should continue the lake and building rhythms through the photographic fragments, dry-printed into cream paper, with broken coverage rather than a decorative blue rectangle. Build TWO irregular, convincingly hand-torn horizontal material handoffs crossing the composition near 38% and 72% of image height, with narrow exposed cream fibers. These divide an upper sky/roof band, a middle architecture band, and a lower water/reflection foreground band that can later be separated for parallax. The three bands must align as ONE continuous complete scene; no gaps, no duplicated buildings, no inset photographs, no collage of separate views.

Calm tactile paper, soft natural colors in photographic regions, one added cobalt hue, modest charcoal detail, visible but restrained print grain. No glossy rendering, 3D paper shadows, curled corners, perspective mockup, thick frames, stickers, decorative labels, intense noise, unrelated objects or watermark. Source-derived torn-paper art, not a photograph with a vintage filter.
