# Contact collage transition

## Reference inspected

Shopify Winter 2026 public bundles, inspected 2026-09-24:

- [Background shader bundle](https://cdn.shopify.com/oxygen-v2/47215/49013/102837/4351350/assets/Background-CGKUhMwd.js): animated noise and mud-normal distortion, progress-driven whole-scene mask, edge glow, Bloom.
- [Particle bundle](https://cdn.shopify.com/oxygen-v2/47215/49013/102837/4351350/assets/Butterflies-DLjrCfBq.js): independent point sprites with velocity, turbulence, size variation and blink uniforms.

The paper overlay shader has no mouse uniform. Mouse influence occurs in the hero circular transition; local pointer response in this implementation is an additional interaction, not a claim about Shopify's paper overlay.

## Local implementation

`liquidContour.ts` solves a moving two-dimensional threshold field instead of translating a fixed wavy line. Broad and medium lobes change shape as scroll progress crosses the field, so different parts of the boundary advance at different speeds. Position-dependent drag and damped springs add local lag and release when scrolling stops or reverses. Continuous time drives gentle motion at rest; a smoothed pointer displaces the nearby edge. The field remains monotonic vertically to prevent contour jumps or disconnected hit regions, and inertia fades at the transition endpoints. Deformation around the travelling baseline (broad lobes, fine ripples and inertial drag) is scaled to 50%; local convergence-spacing variation is also halved. Scroll travel and time rates are unchanged.

`createHandoffs.ts` shares the resulting samples between the SVG image mask, DOM hit-test clip and GPU glow. Per-sample velocity also drives the dust's short trailing motion, sideways drift and restrained brightness response. The contact handoff uses the field for vertical travel; the directory star flow described below recentres the same field around its diagonal boundary.

`createChapterEdge.ts` now shares `portalLight.ts` with the circular reveal: cold-white emission `(0.9, 0.96, 1)`, exponential band/filament falloffs `40/230`, the same star field and blinking, and the same noise breakup. It uses the portal's two-pass seven-tap blur (step 2.4), Bloom contribution 0.65 and glow-alpha response 0.9 through the shared composite shader. Independent drifting point sprites remain, now in the same cold-white palette. DOM content cannot supply a scene-luminance contour sample to this overlay, so that optional contribution is zero; all light-kernel parameters are shared. The fixed HalfFloat emission targets retain HDR highlights, matching the opening scene's WebGL 2 / EXT_color_buffer_float requirement. This is an original implementation of the observed mechanisms; no Shopify code or textures are copied into the project.

The contact handoff also samples its live rendered collage before masking. `contact/transitionField.ts` transfers the exact seam samples and smoothed pointer to the shared picture-detail shader; `createContactGhost.ts` extracts mountain and crystal facets from the current framebuffer and applies the shared contour echoes and six-sample pointer wake only below the seam. Above the seam, `contact/departureDetails.ts` lets the outgoing work and skills renderers extract their own picture details, using the same live seam and pointer samples. Neither source paints its details across the boundary. A separate DOM content wrapper retains the SVG paint mask and polygon hit clip; the canvas applies its own matching coverage without blocking outgoing links. The contact texture-detail response is halved to avoid overexposing the denser rock facets. The shared `textDetailTexture.ts` cache also captures the contact DOM lettering at its actual glyph positions, so the lower-side detail source does not disappear when the seam rises from the crystal to the heading and description. Hidden copy is excluded and ancestor opacity is respected; the cache refreshes for layout, visibility or font changes. Text and picture details use the same 150px band, side coverage and pointer wake; text does not leak into the outgoing page. The contact crystal now reuses the opening GLB, sampled point buffer, lighting map, rigid pointer rotation and particle-motion/depth kernel through `createContactCrystal.ts`. It is rendered with a perspective camera into depth/light and sharp-particle targets, then composited transparently between the landscape and foreground in the existing contact renderer. The point composite enters the same framebuffer detail pass, so the seam clips and echoes the actual moving particles. The old crystal cutout and five painted fragment layers are removed; the non-WebGL/loading background uses the crystal-free landscape. Offscreen targets resize with the canvas and are released with the scene; rendering is paused with the existing contact visibility clock. The extra pass runs only during the contact handoff, fades at both endpoints, and its framebuffer and uniforms are disposed with the scene. Reduced motion skips the handoff; the painted fallback keeps its original mask.

The seam travels from bottom to top as the user scrolls down. The outgoing directory stays fully opaque above it; the contact composition is revealed below it, without an intermediate blank-paper fade. The image and typography remain one masked composition. There are no image strips or electrical arcs. Contact uses the opening's rigid particle model, while its landscape and foreground retain their existing scroll motion.

The work/skills star flow uses the same `createChapterEdge` renderer and the evolving `liquidContour` field. During career-to-directory entry, two contours arrive from the upper-left and lower-right, revealing the incoming directory on their outer sides while the career page remains between them. Position-dependent spacing varies during convergence, giving different segments different travel speeds. The spacing remains positive and reaches exactly zero; the merged seam continues evolving under the same clock, without resetting the field or stopping its broad lobes. Each side supplies its own velocity to the particle renderer. Reduced motion freezes this time-driven deformation.

Both contours, the SVG mask and hit regions use the same samples. The work/skills shaders now read those CPU samples through per-renderer textures instead of maintaining a second analytic curve; image softening therefore follows the same moving geometry. These textures are disposed with their renderer. The work/skills image blend is confined to the star seam and click regions are retained; expanding either destination fades the star seam and closing restores it.

During convergence, both picture shaders also extract luminance derivatives from the sharp current scene before applying the narrow image blur. A sparse, blinking breakup of those actual picture details is mixed into a 150 CSS pixel band around the moving seams. This produces luminous texture fragments attached to the scene's architecture, artwork and skill tiles instead of an unrelated star overlay. Automatic detail lighting fades out over the final 18% of convergence. A shared six-sample pointer wake locally strengthens the details and remains available on the merged seam; it eases away after pointer leave, window blur or touch input, and is disabled for reduced motion. Each picture uses its own contour distance. GPU coverage clips both the original picture and its detail fragments to the same side. Work and skills never export their picture details across the seam or into the white career gap. Dynamic canvases bypass the parent SVG paint mask while DOM labels, static fallback and hit regions retain it. Opening either destination fades the effect completely. The source picture is dimmed by at most 32% locally under combined hover and transition. No previous-frame feedback buffer or whole-page ghost image is used.

Image softening is always attached to the two moving contours: each has a 20px influence band with a 7px sampling radius. The union uses nearest-edge distance, so merging does not double the blur. The entry mask feathers the combined reveal by 3.5px on both sides, while the glow canvas remains outside that mask. The same narrow softening continues on the final star seam without a delayed blur toggle. CPU mask/glow geometry and both picture shaders share the curve, pointer deformation and convergence separation. The reduced-motion skills view uses the same narrow feather. The experimental thick rim and dense grain overlay have been removed; the original soft star edge is restored.

The circular portal also receives the seam's interaction response: exponential pointer smoothing at rate 9, local tangential/normal influence radii of 105/135 CSS pixels, up to 24px rim displacement, and 20px star-field repulsion. Its centre stays fixed. Both coverage and glow use the same deformed distance field. Scroll speed briefly increases fine contour movement, with the same 1400px/s reference and 14/3 attack/decay rates as the seam. Touch input does not retain hover; blur/leave fade the pointer response, and reduced motion disables it. The light kernel and opening timeline are unchanged by this interaction. The circular reveal now also uses the shared `pictureDetail.ts` contour extraction and sparse breakup. It samples the outgoing crystal before blackout, maps the incoming career image to its live DOM rectangle, and samples a cached text-contour texture positioned with DOM glyph ranges. That text texture is redrawn only when layout or fonts change, with GPU storage recreated on size changes; it makes narrow-screen portals respond even before the career image enters view. A six-sample pointer wake strengthens details within a 150px band on both sides of the same deformed circular rim. Source ownership is independent: only crystal details are emitted outside the hole, and only career image/text details are emitted inside it. The sources are never combined before masking. Silver details appear over black, subdued gray details over white; the DOM stays responsible for the readable content. This detail layer bypasses Bloom, so the accepted rim light is unchanged. Reveal completion clears the whole overlay. Image/text textures, font listeners and pointer resources are released with the opening scene.

Rendering is limited to the active handoff and paused when the document is hidden. Reduced-motion preference bypasses the handoff. Point count is 900 on desktop and 420 below 600 CSS pixels; renderer DPR is capped at 1.5. All listeners and GPU resources are released on disposal.

## Input during handoffs

Opening a destination during a handoff first completes the existing composition: during the contact reveal, the same scroll-driven seam retreats downward until contact is fully out of view; during career entry, the two directory seams finish gathering. Only then are handoffs suspended and the selected work/skills view expanded. Lenis input is paused during this short sequence, but the live contour, particles and interactive detail rendering keep running. The pending open is guarded against repeated clicks, and Escape or disposal cancels the preparation tween before it can expand a stale destination.

Holding a chapter for a transition no longer makes it inert. Work and skills buttons use the exact sampled upper/lower star contours, including convergence and pointer displacement, as their hit-test clip paths. The empty area between them passes input through to the visible career links. Contact uses its sampled reveal contour for hit-test clipping as well as its SVG paint mask, so its transparent area cannot block the outgoing directory. Loading and fullscreen-view inert states remain owned by their components.

Pointer capture keeps the destination selected at press time if the edge moves before release. Movement beyond 10 CSS pixels or pointer cancellation suppresses activation, while keyboard activation remains available. Closing either destination always returns to the complete directory at the explore chapter start. Before the fixed view contracts, the paused Lenis position is synchronously rebased with immediate/force, chapter masks remain cleared and gather is set to one. Scrolling resumes only after closing finishes, so the previous partial entry/contact handoff cannot flash back. The return button, Escape and browser-history close share this path.

## Chapter rail

The rail's adjacent labels now share continuous expansion weights from the scene: crystal camera travel, circular portal reveal, career-to-hub gather progress and hub-to-contact collage progress. Width, text opacity and fill opacity use those weights directly, with no timed width/opacity transitions or hover expansion to override a paused frame. Reading fill progress is local to each chapter. The current-step accessibility marker follows the dominant chapter independently of the visual interpolation. Reduced motion retains discrete chapter selection. Expanded widths are measured from the labels and refreshed for viewport/font changes; all observers and listeners are disposed with the main timeline.
