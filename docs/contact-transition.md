The paper overlay shader has no mouse uniform. Mouse influence occurs in the hero circular transition; local pointer response in this implementation is an additional interaction, not a claim about Shopify's paper overlay.

## Local implementation

The current contact artwork uses two independent oil-painted mountain cutouts over a near-black sky and a procedural animated black hole. It keeps one persistent contact invitation. See [contact-black-hole-design.md](contact-black-hole-design.md) for the layer motion, fallback and asset prompts. The seam and source-owned interactive detail pipeline below still apply.

`liquidContour.ts` solves a moving two-dimensional threshold field instead of translating a fixed wavy line. Broad and medium lobes change shape as scroll progress crosses the field, so different parts of the boundary advance at different speeds. Position-dependent drag and damped springs add local lag and release when scrolling stops or reverses. Continuous time drives gentle motion at rest; a smoothed pointer displaces the nearby edge. The field remains monotonic vertically to prevent contour jumps or disconnected hit regions, and inertia fades at the transition endpoints. Deformation around the travelling baseline (broad lobes, fine ripples and inertial drag) is scaled to 50%; local convergence-spacing variation is also halved. Scroll travel and time rates are unchanged.

`createHandoffs.ts` shares the resulting samples between the SVG image mask, DOM hit-test clip and GPU glow. Per-sample velocity also drives the dust's short trailing motion, sideways drift and restrained brightness response. The contact handoff uses this field for vertical travel; the directory entry uses the circular portal described below.

`createChapterEdge.ts` now shares `portalLight.ts` with the circular reveal: cold-white emission `(0.9, 0.96, 1)`, exponential band/filament falloffs `40/230`, the same star field and blinking, and the same noise breakup. It uses the portal's two-pass seven-tap blur (step 2.4), Bloom contribution 0.65 and glow-alpha response 0.9 through the shared composite shader. Independent drifting point sprites remain, now in the same cold-white palette. DOM content cannot supply a scene-luminance contour sample to this overlay, so that optional contribution is zero; all light-kernel parameters are shared. The fixed HalfFloat emission targets retain HDR highlights, matching the opening scene's WebGL 2 / EXT_color_buffer_float requirement. This is an original implementation of the observed mechanisms; no Shopify code or textures are copied into the project.

The contact handoff samples its live rendered scene before masking. `contact/transitionField.ts` transfers the exact seam samples and smoothed pointer to the shared picture-detail shader; `createContactGhost.ts` extracts details from the black hole, background particles and foreground mountains, applying contour echoes and the six-sample pointer wake only below the seam. Above the seam, `contact/departureDetails.ts` lets the outgoing work and skills renderers extract their own picture details using the same live seam and pointer samples. Neither source paints across the boundary. A separate DOM content wrapper retains the SVG paint mask and polygon hit clip; the canvas applies matching coverage without blocking outgoing links. The contact texture-detail response is halved to avoid overexposing the rock facets.

The shared `textDetailTexture.ts` cache captures contact lettering at its actual glyph positions, so details remain available as the seam rises to the heading and description. Hidden copy is excluded and ancestor opacity is respected; the cache refreshes for layout, visibility or font changes. Text and picture details use the same 150px band, side coverage and pointer wake. Text does not leak into the outgoing page. The extra pass runs only during the handoff and fades at both endpoints. Its targets resize with the canvas and are disposed with the scene. Reduced motion skips the handoff; the static fallback retains its matching mask.

The seam travels from bottom to top as the user scrolls down. The outgoing directory stays fully opaque above it; the contact composition is revealed below it without an intermediate blank-paper fade. The image and typography remain one masked composition. There are no image strips or electrical arcs.

The current contact scene composites a fixed-camera white particle background, the animated black hole, and two independent mountain cutouts. It draws one fifth of the original ambient points (2,400 of 12,000); crystal surface points are excluded. Black-hole coverage and mountain alpha occlude the background particles before the scene reaches the handoff pass. The background's natural drift continues independently of black-hole orbit and mountain parallax. Current artwork, layer parameters, fallback and provenance are documented in the final contact design above.

Career-to-directory entry now uses one expanding circular hole. `portalContour.ts` samples the opening portal's radius and broad/fine noise field on the CPU; progress also owns its drift so reverse scrolling retraces the same edge. One polygon clip on `.hub-stage` reveals the artwork, labels, white mat and frame together. The same radial samples drive `createChapterEdge.ts`, reusing the opening's light kernel, dust sprites and Bloom without creating another renderer. The two-corner convergence, its SVG entry mask/feather and its separation function have been removed.

The directory's fixed diagonal and frame use the separate ink boundary documented in [explore-frame-study.md](explore-frame-study.md). They no longer move during chapter entry. Contact still uses its original liquid seam and source-owned picture-detail effects.

The opening crystal-to-career circular portal also receives the seam's interaction response: exponential pointer smoothing at rate 9, local tangential/normal influence radii of 105/135 CSS pixels, up to 24px rim displacement, and 20px star-field repulsion. Its centre stays fixed. Both coverage and glow use the same deformed distance field. Scroll speed briefly increases fine contour movement, with the same 1400px/s reference and 14/3 attack/decay rates as the seam. Touch input does not retain hover; blur/leave fade the pointer response, and reduced motion disables it. The light kernel and opening timeline are unchanged by this interaction. The circular reveal now also uses the shared `pictureDetail.ts` contour extraction and sparse breakup. It samples the outgoing crystal before blackout, maps the incoming career image to its live DOM rectangle, and samples a cached text-contour texture positioned with DOM glyph ranges. That text texture is redrawn only when layout or fonts change, with GPU storage recreated on size changes; it makes narrow-screen portals respond even before the career image enters view. A six-sample pointer wake strengthens details within a 150px band on both sides of the same deformed circular rim. Source ownership is independent: only crystal details are emitted outside the hole, and only career image/text details are emitted inside it. The sources are never combined before masking. Silver details appear over black, subdued gray details over white; the DOM stays responsible for the readable content. This detail layer bypasses Bloom, so the accepted rim light is unchanged. Reveal completion clears the whole overlay. Image/text textures, font listeners and pointer resources are released with the opening scene.

Rendering is limited to the active handoff and paused when the document is hidden. Reduced-motion preference bypasses the handoff. Point count is 900 on desktop and 420 below 600 CSS pixels; renderer DPR is capped at 1.5. All listeners and GPU resources are released on disposal.

## Input during handoffs

Opening a destination during a handoff first completes the existing composition: during the contact reveal, the same scroll-driven seam retreats downward until contact is fully out of view; during career entry, the same circular hole finishes opening. Only then are handoffs suspended and the selected work/skills view expanded. Lenis input is paused during this short sequence, but the live contour, particles and interactive detail rendering keep running. The pending open is guarded against repeated clicks, and Escape or disposal cancels the preparation tween before it can expand a stale destination.

Holding a chapter for a transition no longer makes it inert. Work and skills buttons use the fixed diagonal and inset frame as their hit-test clips. The whole-stage portal clip also restricts hit testing, so points outside the hole reach the visible career links. Contact uses its sampled reveal contour for hit-test clipping as well as its SVG paint mask, so its transparent area cannot block the outgoing directory. Loading and fullscreen-view inert states remain owned by their components.

Pointer capture keeps the destination selected at press time if the edge moves before release. Movement beyond 10 CSS pixels or pointer cancellation suppresses activation, while keyboard activation remains available. Closing either destination always returns to the complete directory at the explore chapter start. Before the fixed view contracts, the paused Lenis position is synchronously rebased with immediate/force, chapter masks remain cleared and gather is set to one. Scrolling resumes only after closing finishes, so the previous partial entry/contact handoff cannot flash back. The return button, Escape and browser-history close share this path.

## Chapter rail

The rail has four chapters: intro (including the crystal), career, explore and contact. Selecting intro returns to the opening start; its fill progress spans the complete opening timeline. The adjacent labels share continuous expansion weights from the scene: circular portal reveal, career-to-hub gather progress and hub-to-contact collage progress. Width, text opacity and fill opacity use those weights directly, with no timed width/opacity transitions or hover expansion to override a paused frame. Reading fill progress is local to each chapter. The current-step accessibility marker follows the dominant chapter independently of the visual interpolation. Reduced motion retains discrete chapter selection. Expanded widths are measured from the labels and refreshed for viewport/font changes; all observers and listeners are disposed with the main timeline.
